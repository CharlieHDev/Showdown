# ⚔️ MC Showdown

A custom-coded 32-player Minecraft tournament where 8 teams of 4 battle across an assortment of unique minigames to claim the most points and be crowned champions.

> **Disclaimer:** MC Showdown is not associated or affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.

## What is Showdown?

Showdown is a competitive Minecraft event designed to bring together creators of any size. Teams compete across **7 custom minigames**, each awarding points — the top two teams then battle in a finale minigame to claim the win.

Beyond the main event, Showdown also hosts **special community events** where viewers can watch their favourite creators play or help raise money for charity.

---

## Games

Our games aim to create a unique and enjoyable experience for all players, combining competitive elements with fun, innovative additions. Each game focuses on a variety of different skills:

- **Movement** — Agility and traversal challenges
- **PvP Ability** — Combat-focused gameplay
- **Puzzle Solving** — Active thinking and problem solving
- **Teamwork** — Coordinated team play
- *Some games combine multiple skills*
